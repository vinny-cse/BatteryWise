OUR PURPOSE

Recycle more. Waste less.

BatteryWise is an educational digital prototype. It demonstrates battery registration and manual status tracking to encourage responsible collection, reuse assessment and recycling. Recycling can reduce demand for some virgin materials, but it does not eliminate the need for mining.
